# Bounded Literature Check

Date: 2026-07-29

## Primary Source

Teng Zhang, *A Matrix Extension of the Arithmetic-Geometric Mean
Inequality*, Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra 34 (2018), 283-287:

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.5058
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.13001/1081-3810.3555

Conjecture 4.1 is the four-matrix operator-norm inequality tested here.
The paper reports numerical evidence for the conjecture.

## Context

- Recht and Re, *Toward a Noncommutative Arithmetic-Geometric Mean
  Inequality: Conjectures, Case-studies, and Consequences*:
  https://proceedings.mlr.press/v23/recht12.html
- Lai and Lim, *Recht-Re Noncommutative Arithmetic-Geometric Mean
  Conjecture is False*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01510

The latter disproves the broader Recht-Re conjecture from five matrices
onward. It also reports that the full \((m,n)=(4,4)\) Recht-Re case passes
its SDP check. Zhang's Conjecture 4.1 is a stronger termwise estimate
proposed as a sufficient route to that full four-matrix result. The
counterexample here blocks Zhang's proposed route; it does not refute the
full \((4,4)\) inequality.
